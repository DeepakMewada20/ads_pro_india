Common Questions

Everything You Want
to Know — Answered.

No fluff. Straight answers to the questions every business owner asks before working with us.

Do you work with small businesses and low budgets?
+
Yes — our ₹999 setup plan is built for businesses just starting with paid ads. We've generated results for clients spending as little as ₹15,000/month on ad spend. The strategy scales with your budget.
How long before I start seeing results?
+
Most clients see their first leads within 3–7 days of launch. Meaningful ROAS improvements typically appear within 2–4 weeks as we gather data and optimise. We launch fast — campaigns go live within 3–5 business days of onboarding.
Which industries do you specialise in?
+
We work across real estate, e-commerce, online coaching, education, D2C brands, and local service businesses. Lead generation is our core strength — if your business depends on inbound enquiries, we can help.
Do you manage the ad account or just set it up?
+
Both — Plan 01 is a one-time setup for businesses who want to manage ads themselves. Plans 02 and 03 include full monthly management: campaign optimisation, A/B testing, budget reallocation, creative refreshes, and weekly reports.
Will I have access to my own ad account?
+
Always. Your ad account is 100% yours. We work as a partner with access — never as an owner. All data, audiences, and assets belong to you. You can see everything in real time, anytime.
What's included in the free strategy call?
+
A 30-minute 1-on-1 session with Gautam. We audit your current ads (or why you haven't started), identify your top 3 growth opportunities, and outline a custom plan — with zero obligation to sign up afterwards.
Are there any hidden fees or ad spend markups?
+
Zero. The price you see is what you pay us. Your ad budget goes directly to Meta or Google — we never touch it or mark it up. Full transparency on every rupee, every month.
Can I cancel anytime on the monthly plans?
+
Yes. There are no lock-in contracts. We earn your business every month through results. If you're not happy after 30 days, you can cancel with no penalties — though in 5 years, it's rarely happened.
